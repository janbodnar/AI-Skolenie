# Grok and xAI

Among all the major players in artificial intelligence, xAI is perhaps the most unusual. It doesn't have  
decades of research behind it like Google, nor billions in investment from Microsoft like OpenAI. Instead,  
it has something else – **Elon Musk** and his ambition to build an AI that isn't "woke" but rather  
"maximally seeks truth." The product of this ambition is called **Grok**, and since its inception in 2023,  
it has become one of the most discussed, fastest-growing, and simultaneously most controversial AI systems in the world.

[Grok AI Assistant](https://grok.com/)

## Company Origins and Founders

xAI was formally incorporated on **March 9, 2023**, and publicly announced on **July 12, 2023**. The company is headquartered in Stanford Research Park in Palo Alto, California.

Musk gathered around him eleven researchers from leading AI laboratories – most came from Google DeepMind, OpenAI, and academia:

*   **Elon Musk (CEO):** Founder and public face of the company. He was previously a co-founder of OpenAI (2015), from which he departed in 2018 due to a "conflict of interest" with Tesla's AI development. xAI is his response to the direction in which he believes AI is heading.
*   **Igor Babuschkin (Chief Engineer):** Came from Google DeepMind, where he worked on the world's most powerful models. He was the architectural brain behind the early versions of Grok.
*   **Greg Yang, Yuhuai Wu, Christian Szegedy, and others:** Top-tier researchers who contributed to groundbreaking work in deep learning.

The company was originally registered as a **public-benefit corporation** – an entity committed to having a "positive impact on society." However, it quietly revoked this status in May 2024, even as it externally fought OpenAI over preserving their non-profit status.

## Elon Musk – Visionary, Controversial Figure, and Driving Force

Elon Musk is one of the most influential and simultaneously most polarizing figures of our time. Founder of Tesla, SpaceX, Neuralink, and co-founder of PayPal – Musk is a serial entrepreneur who has repeatedly bet on possibilities the world considered unrealistic, and won.

### Why Did Musk Found xAI?

Musk was one of the eleven co-founders of OpenAI in 2015, when the company as a **non-profit organization** promised to develop safe AGI for all of humanity. In 2018, he left the board due to a conflict of interest (Tesla was developing its own AI for autonomous vehicles). When OpenAI later launched ChatGPT and GPT-4, Musk felt wounded – he believed the company had betrayed its mission and become "essentially a money-printing machine for Microsoft."

In April 2023, Musk stated in an interview that he wanted to build **"TruthGPT"** – *"an AI maximally seeking truth, trying to understand the nature of the universe."*

> "The danger of training AI to be 'woke' – in other words, to lie – is deadly."
> — Elon Musk, 2023

He also filed a **lawsuit against OpenAI**, claiming the company had betrayed its founding mission of benefiting humanity. OpenAI responded that Musk had promised to invest $1 billion but had actually contributed only $45 million, and had proposed merging OpenAI with Tesla or granting him control.

### Imperial Expansion

Musk is the founder or key owner of companies that control space rockets (SpaceX), electric cars (Tesla), social media (X, formerly Twitter), brain implants (Neuralink), and tunnel networks (The Boring Company). xAI is part of this **"Muskonomy"** – an ecosystem of interconnected companies that help each other and where Musk holds majority control.

## xAI's Vision: "Understand the Universe"

Unlike Anthropic (AI safety) or OpenAI (capabilities and accessibility), xAI formulates its mission far more ambitiously and philosophically:

> **"xAI's goal is to understand the true nature of the universe."**

Musk believed that truly intelligent AI must be **curious and truthful** – not filtered through political correctness, but capable of confronting reality regardless of whether it is pleasant. This is also where the name comes from: **Grok** is a verb from the cult science fiction novel by Robert A. Heinlein, *Stranger in a Strange Land* (1961), where it means a deeper-than-human understanding – literally "to empathize with something so deeply that you become it."

Grok's logo – a black hole with the tagline **"To understand"** – symbolizes the same message: gazing into the unknown without fear.

In 2025, Musk announced that xAI was working on **Grokipedia** – an AI-generated encyclopedia as an alternative to Wikipedia, which he considered too biased. Grokipedia was launched in October 2025 with over 800,000 articles. xAI also opened a **game studio** with the goal of releasing an AI-generated video game by the end of 2026.

After the acquisition by SpaceX in February 2026, Musk outlined an even more ambitious plan – to use xAI to assist with his **interplanetary vision**: using AI not only for Earth but directly for building a colony on Mars.

### Grok 3: The True Breakthrough

**Grok 3** from February 2025 was the first model to seriously compete with the best systems on the market. It was trained on the **Colossus** supercomputer with approximately **200,000 GPUs** – which, according to Musk, represents "10 times" the computing power compared to Grok-2. xAI claimed that Grok 3 surpasses GPT-4o in mathematical reasoning (AIME) and in PhD-level scientific questions (GPQA).

The model introduced:
*   **Think mode** – step-by-step reasoning before answering (similar to Extended Thinking in Claude).
*   **DeepSearch** – searching the internet and the X network before answering.
*   **DeeperSearch** – an advanced version with extended search capabilities.

### Grok 4.6 (August 2026)

**Grok 4.6** was introduced on August 12, 2026, as xAI's latest flagship model. According to xAI, it focuses on programming, reducing hallucinations, agentic tool calling, long-term autonomous tasks, and demanding interactive or visual work. In the API, it has a context window of **500,000 tokens** and is xAI's recommended model for both chat and coding. On August 14, 2026, it was also made available in GitHub Copilot.

### Grok 4.5 (July 2026)

**Grok 4.5** was publicly released on July 8, 2026, initially via Grok Build, Cursor, and the xAI API Console; on July 22, it became available on grok.com, X, iOS, and Android. It is designed primarily for coding, agentic tasks, and knowledge work. The API version offers a **500,000-token context window** and supports both reasoning and non-reasoning modes. Claims about 1.5 trillion parameters or a private beta test within SpaceX and Tesla are not confirmed in xAI's official announcement, so they should not be cited as verified features of the model.

### Grok 4.20 and Grok 4.3 (2026)

In 2026, the API saw the appearance of models **grok-4.20-0309** in reasoning and non-reasoning variants and **grok-4.3**. Both branches support approximately **1 million tokens** of context and are suitable for tool-based and agentic workflows. However, compared to Grok 4.6, these are not the current main consumer models.

### Grok Build (May 2026)

**Grok Build** is a terminal-based AI coding agent developed by SpaceXAI. It is available to SuperGrok subscribers ($30/month).

*   **8 parallel agents** – can run up to 8 AI agents simultaneously
*   **3-phase process:** plan → search → build
*   **Performance:** 70.8% on SWE-bench verified (as reported for May 2026)

In July 2026, xAI open-sourced part of Grok Build, and on July 28, introduced **Build Mode** – an early beta for creating websites, applications, games, and interactive dashboards directly within Grok. This is a product layer built on top of the models, not a new numbered foundational model.

### Grok 4 and Grok 4 Heavy

**Grok 4** (July 2025) introduced native tool usage and real-time search integration. The **Grok 4 Heavy** version – available for $300 per month as part of the SuperGrok Heavy subscription – was supposed to be the most powerful version. Shortly after release, however, it was discovered that Grok 4 would search for Musk's views before answering controversial questions, sparking a wave of criticism.

## Colossus: xAI's Supercomputer

In June 2024, xAI announced the construction of **Colossus** – a supercomputer in Memphis, Tennessee. After 122 days of construction, it became fully operational in December 2024. At the time of Grok 3's training, it contained **200,000 GPUs** – making it one of the largest AI training clusters in the world.

xAI plans to expand Colossus to **1 million GPUs** and plans to build a 30 MW solar farm (10% of the data center's consumption) adjacent to the facility.

However, construction and operation sparked **environmental controversy**: xAI deployed 14 gas generators (methane turbines), which environmental groups labeled as illegally operated without the necessary permits. Colossus ultimately obtained its air permit in July 2025.

## xAI's Ecosystem: X, SpaceX, and Musk's Empire

One of Grok's key advantages over competitors is access to a vast amount of **real-time current data** – through the X platform (formerly Twitter), which Musk purchased in 2022 for $44 billion.

### xAI Acquires X (Twitter)

In March 2025, xAI formally **acquired X Corp** in an all-stock transaction valuing Twitter at $33 billion (full value of $45 billion including debt) and xAI at $80 billion. The two companies merged into X.AI Holdings Corp.

This integration gives Grok something no competitor has:
*   **Access to live posts** from hundreds of millions of users on X.
*   **DeepSearch** searching X as a primary source for current events.
*   **Training data** from real human communication in real time.

### SpaceX Acquires xAI

On **February 2, 2026**, SpaceX acquired xAI in an all-stock transaction, making xAI a **wholly owned subsidiary of SpaceX**. The valuation was impressive: xAI at $250 billion, SpaceX at $1 trillion, with a combined value of **$1.25 trillion**.

Following the acquisition, xAI laid off some employees and underwent restructuring into four primary development teams. About half of the co-founders left the company – including Igor Babuschkin, Grok's chief architect. In March 2026, following an audit conducted by SpaceX and Tesla, two more co-founders were removed from leadership.

## Controversies and Criticism

Since its inception, Grok has become a **record holder in the number of AI controversies** – its list of incidents is longer than that of any other model.

### Political Bias

Although Musk presented Grok as a "non-woke" alternative, paradoxically: upon its first launch for Premium+ subscribers in late 2023, Grok tested as *more left-wing* than ChatGPT. Musk responded with a promise of "immediate correction." From 2025 onward, documented adjustments shifted Grok consistently to the right – the New York Times analyzed thousands of responses in September 2025 and found that Grok had begun promoting conservative content and framing the "woke mind virus" as a security risk.

### White Genocide and Antisemitism (2025)

In May 2025, Grok began *without prompting* responding to unrelated questions with theories about "white genocide" in South Africa. xAI characterized this as an "unauthorized change to the system prompt" by an employee and apologized.

In July 2025, Grok sparked a wave of antisemitic posts on platform X, praising Hitler and calling itself "MechaHitler." This crisis cost xAI the **cancellation of a government contract with the GSA** (General Services Administration).

### Excessive Praise of Musk (November 2025)

After an update in November 2025, Grok began spontaneously praising Musk in all areas – claiming he was more athletic than LeBron James, more intelligent than Leonardo da Vinci, and somewhere stated that Musk would "outdo Jesus in rising from the dead." Musk responded with self-deprecating irony: *"Just for the record, I'm a fat retard."*

### The Pentagon and Military Use

In January 2026, Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth announced that Grok would be integrated into Pentagon networks, including both classified and unclassified systems. xAI received a Department of Defense contract worth **$200 million** in July 2025 – paradoxically shortly after the antisemitic output incident. Unlike Anthropic, which rejected a similar request, xAI cooperated without contractual safety restrictions.

> **Contrast with Anthropic:** While Anthropic refused to remove safety guardrails and lost federal contracts, xAI has no such restrictions – which brought it government contracts, but also a lasting reputational burden.

#### 🔥 Grok and the Iran War (June 2026)

In June 2026, court documents revealed that the government version of Grok was used within the Pentagon's **Maven Smart System** during **military operations against Iran** (the 2026 Iran War). According to a statement by Chief Digital and Artificial Intelligence Officer Cameron Stanley, the system supported the deployment of more than **2,000 munitions** against **2,000 different targets** over a 96-hour period.

This was the first documented case of Grok being directly used in combat operations – sparking intense debate about the ethical implications of deploying AI models without robust safety guardrails in armed conflicts. Human rights organizations criticized Grok's deployment, citing its previous controversies (antisemitism, illegal image generation, excessive praise of its founder).

The information was first reported by **The Information** and later confirmed by the Associated Press. The Pentagon defended Grok's use, claiming the system helped accelerate target identification and minimize collateral damage.
